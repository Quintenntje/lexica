# Employment Agreement

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 1 April 2025 between **Osterby Robotics Ltd**, a company registered in England and Wales with its registered office at 7 Millbrook Yard, Bristol BS1 6QH, United Kingdom (the "Employer"), and **Jordan Avery**, of 31 Hartcliffe Close, Bristol BS3 5RN (the "Employee"). This document is a fictional sample contract created for demonstration purposes only; the Employer and the Employee are fictional.

## Article 1 — Commencement and Position

1.1 **Start date.** The Employee's employment under this Agreement begins on 2 June 2025 (the "Start Date"). No period of previous employment counts towards the Employee's period of continuous employment.

1.2 **Job title and duties.** The Employee is employed as Senior Firmware Engineer and shall perform the duties reasonably assigned by the Employer that are consistent with that position, reporting to the Head of Embedded Systems.

1.3 **Place of work.** The Employee's normal place of work is the Employer's office in Bristol. The Employee may work from home for up to two (2) days per week in accordance with the Employer's hybrid working policy.

## Article 2 — Probationary Period

2.1 **Length of probation.** The first six (6) months of employment are a probationary period. The Employer may extend the probationary period by up to three (3) months by written notice before it ends.

2.2 **Notice during probation.** During the probationary period, either party may terminate the employment by giving one (1) week's written notice.

## Article 3 — Hours of Work

3.1 **Normal hours.** The Employee's normal working hours are 9:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, with an unpaid lunch break of thirty (30) minutes.

3.2 **Additional hours.** The Employee may be required to work additional hours when necessary for the proper performance of their duties. No additional pay is due for such hours.

## Article 4 — Remuneration and Benefits

4.1 **Salary.** The Employer shall pay the Employee a gross annual salary of GBP 68,000, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on or around the last working day of each month. Salary is reviewed annually in April.

4.2 **Pension.** The Employee will be enrolled in the Employer's workplace pension scheme. The Employer contributes five percent (5%) of the Employee's basic salary.

4.3 **Expenses.** The Employer shall reimburse reasonable expenses properly incurred in performing the Employee's duties, subject to the Employer's expenses policy.

## Article 5 — Holidays and Sickness

5.1 **Annual leave.** The Employee is entitled to twenty-five (25) working days of paid holiday in each holiday year (January to December), in addition to the public holidays in England. Up to five (5) unused days may be carried over to the following year.

5.2 **Sickness absence.** The Employee must notify their manager by 10:00 on the first day of any sickness absence. Absences of more than seven (7) calendar days must be supported by a medical certificate.

## Article 6 — Confidentiality

6.1 **Confidential information.** The Employee shall not, during the employment or at any time after it ends, use or disclose any confidential information of the Employer, including trade secrets, source code, product designs, customer lists and financial information, except in the proper performance of their duties or as required by law.

6.2 **Duration after termination.** The obligation in clause 6.1 continues after termination of employment without limit in time for trade secrets, and for a period of three (3) years after termination for all other confidential information.

6.3 **Return of property.** On termination, or earlier on request, the Employee shall return all Employer property, including laptops, access cards, documents and data, and shall not keep any copies.

## Article 7 — Intellectual Property

7.1 **Ownership of work product.** All intellectual property rights in any inventions, software, designs, documents and other works created by the Employee in the course of employment, whether or not during working hours or using the Employer's equipment, belong to the Employer from the moment of creation.

7.2 **Disclosure and assistance.** The Employee shall promptly disclose to the Employer any invention or work referred to in clause 7.1 and, both during and after employment, shall sign any documents reasonably required to confirm or register the Employer's rights, at the Employer's expense.

7.3 **Prior inventions.** Intellectual property created by the Employee before the Start Date, and listed in Schedule 1, remains the property of the Employee. The Employer receives a royalty-free licence to use any such prior invention that the Employee incorporates into the Employer's products.

## Article 8 — Termination

8.1 **Notice period.** After the probationary period, either party may terminate the employment by giving the other not less than two (2) months' written notice. After five (5) years of continuous employment, the notice required from the Employer increases by one (1) week for each further complete year of service, up to a maximum of twelve (12) weeks.

8.2 **Garden leave.** During any notice period, the Employer may require the Employee not to attend work and not to contact clients or colleagues, while continuing to pay full salary and benefits.

8.3 **Summary dismissal.** The Employer may terminate the employment immediately without notice or payment in lieu of notice if the Employee commits gross misconduct, a serious breach of this Agreement, or is convicted of a criminal offence that affects their ability to perform their duties.

## Article 9 — Restrictive Covenants

9.1 **Non-solicitation.** For six (6) months after termination, the Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit any customer of the Employer with whom the Employee had material dealings during the last twelve (12) months of employment.

9.2 **Non-poaching.** For six (6) months after termination, the Employee shall not induce any senior employee of the Employer to leave their employment.

## Article 10 — Liability

10.1 **Employee liability.** The Employee shall be liable to the Employer for loss caused by the Employee's wilful misconduct or gross negligence. The Employee is not liable for losses caused by ordinary errors made in good faith in the course of their duties.

## Article 11 — Notices

11.1 **Notices.** Any notice under this Agreement must be in writing and may be given by hand or sent by email: to the Employer at its registered office or to the HR department's email address, and to the Employee at their home address or personal email address last notified to the Employer. A notice sent by email is deemed received at 9:00 on the next working day.

## Article 12 — Governing Law and Jurisdiction

12.1 **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.

12.2 **Jurisdiction.** The courts and employment tribunals of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
