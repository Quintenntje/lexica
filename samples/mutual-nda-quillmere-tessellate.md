# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 3 March 2025 (the "Effective Date") between **Quillmere Analytics B.V.**, a private limited company with its registered office at Keizersgracht 418, 1016 GD Amsterdam, the Netherlands ("Quillmere"), and **Tessellate Health Ltd**, a company with its registered office at 22 Larchfield Road, Leeds LS6 4BT, United Kingdom ("Tessellate"). Quillmere and Tessellate are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

The Parties wish to explore a possible collaboration on anonymised patient-flow analytics (the "Purpose") and, in doing so, expect to disclose confidential information to each other. This document is a fictional sample contract created for demonstration purposes only.

## Article 1 — Definitions

1.1 **Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether orally, in writing or in any other form, that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. It includes source code, algorithms, data sets, product roadmaps, pricing, customer lists and business plans.

1.2 **Representatives.** "Representatives" means a Party's directors, officers, employees, professional advisers and subcontractors who need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose.

1.3 **Affiliate.** "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting shares.

## Article 2 — Obligations of the Receiving Party

2.1 **Use restriction.** The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose.

2.2 **Non-disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party other than its Representatives, and shall ensure that its Representatives are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement. The Receiving Party is liable for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

2.3 **Standard of care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

2.4 **Notification of unauthorised disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party in writing without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure of Confidential Information.

## Article 3 — Exclusions

3.1 **Excluded information.** The obligations in Article 2 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was lawfully in its possession before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information.

3.2 **Compelled disclosure.** The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or order of a competent court, provided that it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice (where legally permitted) and discloses only the minimum information required.

## Article 4 — Intellectual Property

4.1 **No licence.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any licence or right in any patent, copyright, trade secret or other intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, except the limited right to use the Confidential Information for the Purpose.

4.2 **Feedback.** Any suggestions or feedback that one Party voluntarily gives about the other Party's products may be used by the recipient without restriction, provided that no Confidential Information of the giving Party is disclosed in doing so.

## Article 5 — Term and Termination

5.1 **Term.** This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for a period of two (2) years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Article 5.

5.2 **Termination for convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party.

5.3 **Survival of confidentiality.** The obligations in Articles 2, 3 and 4 survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years after the date of expiry or termination. Obligations relating to trade secrets survive for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## Article 6 — Return and Destruction

6.1 **Return of materials.** Within fourteen (14) days after termination or a written request from the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall return or securely destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and confirm this in writing.

6.2 **Retained copies.** The Receiving Party may retain copies held in automatic electronic back-ups or required to be retained by law, provided that such copies remain subject to this Agreement for as long as they are retained.

## Article 7 — Liability and Remedies

7.1 **Injunctive relief.** Each Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief or specific performance without proving special damage.

7.2 **Limitation of liability.** Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect or consequential loss arising out of this Agreement. This limitation does not apply to liability for breach of Article 2, fraud, or wilful misconduct.

## Article 8 — Notices

8.1 **Form of notice.** Any notice under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by registered post or by email to the addresses set out at the start of this Agreement (or another address notified in writing).

8.2 **Deemed receipt.** A notice is deemed received: if delivered by hand, on delivery; if sent by registered post, on the third (3rd) business day after posting; and if sent by email, at the time of transmission, unless the sender receives an automated message that the email was not delivered.

## Article 9 — Governing Law and Jurisdiction

9.1 **Governing law.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of the Netherlands.

9.2 **Jurisdiction.** The competent courts of Amsterdam, the Netherlands, have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

## Article 10 — General

10.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions about confidentiality relating to the Purpose.

10.2 **No obligation to proceed.** Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to enter into any further agreement or business relationship.

10.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except to an Affiliate.
